Wear the algorithm that changed deep learning. Nesterov's Momentum is a cornerstone technique in neural network optimization, introduced by Yurii Nesterov in 1983. Unlike standard momentum which calculates gradients at the current position, Nesterov's method looks ahead—computing gradients at a position slightly forward in the direction of accumulated momentum. This anticipatory step allows the optimizer to correct course more responsively, leading to faster convergence with rate O(1/t²) compared to traditional gradient descent's O(1/t).

This design pays homage to the mathematical beauty behind modern AI: the subtle yet powerful update rule that helps navigate loss landscapes with saddle points and high curvature regions.
