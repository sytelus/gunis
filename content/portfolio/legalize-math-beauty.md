e^(iπ) + 1 = 0

Five fundamental constants—zero, one, e, i, and π—united in a single elegant equation. Nobel laureate Richard Feynman called it "the most remarkable formula in mathematics," and a 1988 Mathematical Intelligencer poll voted it the most beautiful feat of all mathematics. This design celebrates that beauty.

Euler's Identity connects the additive identity (0), the multiplicative identity (1), the base of natural logarithms (e ≈ 2.718), the imaginary unit (i = √-1), and the circle constant (π ≈ 3.14159) through nothing more than exponentiation, multiplication, and addition—each appearing exactly once. It's not just an equation; it's a window into the deep structure of mathematics itself.

The Beauty Edition is for those who see poetry in proofs and elegance in equations. Perfect for mathematicians, physicists, engineers, students, and anyone who believes that truth and beauty are inseparable.
