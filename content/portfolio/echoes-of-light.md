Light never truly disappears—it scatters, reflects, refracts, and transforms. This design explores that journey: the way sunlight echoes through clouds, the way candlelight dances on water, the way color persists in memory long after the source has faded. It's a meditation on impermanence and continuity, captured in fabric.

The flowing patterns evoke light in motion: interference patterns, diffraction gradients, the soft blur of light through frosted glass. These are the echoes that light leaves behind as it travels through our world, each surface adding its own voice to the chorus. The effect is simultaneously abstract and familiar, scientific and romantic.

This dress embodies that duality. The lightweight fabric catches actual light as you move, creating a dynamic interplay between the printed pattern and real-world illumination. Perfect for evening events, gallery openings, or any occasion where you want to carry a piece of captured light with you.
